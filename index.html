Does it cry wolf when nothing is wrong?

A monitor that is always checking will eventually see a bump by chance. DriftWatch is built so that, however long it runs, a stream that never changes raises a false alarm at most 5 times in 100. Test it: 100 streams, none of them broken.

About this demo. The two safety judges were trained on a public dataset (PKU-SafeRLHF), and their measured hit and miss rates for each topic drive every simulated reply here. The failure you pick is injected at a known moment, which is how we know the right answer.

From the full benchmark of 160 runs on 35,000 cached replies:

  • On quiet streams DriftWatch false-alarmed 3.0% of the time. The usual rolling z-test did 85.5%.
  • It named the right cause in 78% of drift runs and never named a wrong one.
  • On plain model drift it was slower than simple monitors. It wins on hidden, topic-level drift and on telling you why.
